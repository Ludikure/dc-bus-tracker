# DC Bus Tracker

**When the next bus comes at any stop of Metrobus, Loudoun County Transit, Fairfax Connector and Arlington Transit —
and where that bus is now.** A free iPhone app, with a Home Screen widget.

## Support

Questions, problems or ideas: write to [metronow@proton.me](mailto:metronow@proton.me), or in the app tap the
gear on the map → **Send feedback** (it adds the app's version and your iOS version, which helps).

## How it works

- **Live times.** A time with the green signal mark is the agency's own prediction for a bus it is tracking right now.
- **Scheduled times.** A grey time is the agency's timetable: no bus is being tracked on that trip yet, so it is not on
  the map either.
- **Buses on the map.** Each bus is where its agency last reported it. A bus's own screen says how long ago that was.
- **Leave in….** The bus's minutes less the walk to its stop, counted in a straight line at an easy pace. A guide, not a
  promise.
- **Alerts.** The agencies' own service notices, shown at the stops and routes they name. Notices an agency leaves up
  long after they stopped being true are not shown.

## Where the times come from

Times, routes, stops and alerts are each agency's own public data. The map is Apple's.

- [Metrobus](https://www.wmata.com/service/bus/) — Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority
- [Loudoun County Transit](https://www.loudoun.gov/transit) — Loudoun County, Virginia
- [Fairfax Connector](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/connector) — Fairfax County, Virginia
- [Arlington Transit (ART)](https://www.arlingtontransit.com) — Arlington County, Virginia

DC Bus Tracker is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the Washington Metropolitan
Area Transit Authority, Loudoun County, Fairfax County or Arlington County. Times are the agencies' predictions and
timetables, and they can be wrong. The app is provided as is, without warranty of any kind.
