# Privacy Policy

**DC Bus Tracker** (iPhone) is a free app showing when the next bus comes at a stop in the Washington, DC area, and where
that bus is now. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. This page says what leaves your phone,
and why. It says the same as the Privacy screen in the app (Settings → Privacy).

*Last updated: November 26, 2026.*

## Your location

Location is optional. With it on, your phone uses it to find the stops nearest you, the walk to them and which way you
are facing. It is not sent to us.

Apple's Maps draws the map and, when you tap a walk, gives the directions. Apple handles that under its own privacy
policy.

## When you search for a place

The words you type in the search go to Apple's Maps, which suggests places; a place you tap is looked up there too.
Apple is told the area the app covers, not where you are. A number alone — a stop's or a route's — is not sent. Apple
handles this under its own privacy policy.

## What our server sees

To show times, the app asks our server for the stops, routes and buses on your screen, and the server fetches them from
the agencies. Nothing about you goes to the agencies.

The server's logs can record each request: its time, what was asked for and the network address it came from. The stops
you ask about are usually near you, so a request can show roughly where you are. The logs are kept for up to 7 days to
keep the service running. We do not sell them, share them or combine them with anything else.

## On your phone

Your saved stops and buses, your recent searches — the stops, routes and places you opened from the search — the
agencies you turned off and your other settings stay on this phone. The Home Screen widget reads them there, with your
location if you allow it.

## If you write to us

Feedback goes by email from your own mail app. We see your address and what you write, and use them only to answer you.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing that identifies anyone.

## Changes and contact

If what the app does with your information changes, this page and the app's Privacy screen change with it, and the date
above says when.
